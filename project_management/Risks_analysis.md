### Risk 04 - Last-minute changes creating a new error
</span>

Our project was tested and showcased numerous times throughout its development.  
This allowed us to have it tested by many other students as features were added.

Furthermore, each new feature is tested right after implementation, and the entire  
project has been put to the test just before submission for evaluation, in order to  
avoid any last-minute errors.

<span style="color: gold">

### Risk 05 - Project does not comply with the norm
</span>

As for risk 04, the project was brought up to the norm regularly during its development.  
This saved us from having to resolve dozens of errors simultaneously and allowed us to  
work on a well-designed, readable, and precise program.

Of course, managing and verifying compliance with the norm are part of our  
pre-submission checks, just as it is for risk 04.
